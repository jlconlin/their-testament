Their Testament Source

A keepsake, not an app

The scriptures they marked, kept as a book.

Their Testament gathers a lifetime of Gospel Library highlights, margin notes, and tags into a typeset book — every verse they marked shown in the color they chose, every note beside it, in their own words.

What the book keeps

The person, on the page.

Their marks, their colors

Every highlighted verse appears in full, with the exact words marked and the exact color — yellow, blue, red — carried through to the page.

Their words, untouched

Notes are set in the margin beside the verse that prompted them, exactly as they were written. Nothing is corrected, shortened, or rephrased.

Ordered like scripture

Old Testament through General Conference, in canonical order, with running heads, a table of contents, and an index of every tag they used.

A closer look

Built to be read, not just stored.

When several notes land on one verse, they stack in reading order and are lettered — a, b — so you always know which note belongs to which phrase. Verses that were skipped leave a quiet gap rather than a wall of unmarked text.

The result is a book you can sit down with: the shape of someone's study, chapter by chapter, in a form that lasts.

How it works

Two steps. Nothing leaves your computer.

Export your annotations

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.
    Export my annotations Drag it up to your bookmarks bar — don't click it here.
  2. Go to scriptures.churchofjesuschrist.org (or any page under Study) and sign in if you aren't already.
  3. Click the bookmark you just saved.

It reads your notes using the session you're already in and saves them as a single file — right to your downloads folder.

Make the book In progress

The in-browser generator — upload your file, choose a few options, download a print-ready PDF — is being built now. Until it's ready, keep your export file somewhere safe; it's the whole record.

Privacy

That it stays yours is the whole point.

The export runs on the Church's own website, in your own browser, using the login you already have. Your annotations are handed straight to your downloads folder and go nowhere else.

  • No account to create, and no password is ever asked for or seen.
  • Nothing is uploaded here. This site is a folder of static files on GitHub — there is no server to receive anything.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no trackers. The code is open — read every line.

Why this exists

Made for one family, shared for anyone's.

Their Testament began as a way to preserve one person's lifetime of scripture study — thousands of highlights and handwritten-feeling notes that lived only inside an app. Turned into a book, they became something you could hand to a grandchild.

It's offered here in case your family has the same thing worth keeping.

Questions

Good things to ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy and no account. The tools are open source. If you print and bind the finished book, that's the only cost, and it's paid to a printer, not here.

Will my notes be edited or cleaned up?

No. A person's own words are never altered — not shortened, corrected, or rephrased. The only change is turning the app's formatting (bold, italics, lists) into typeset formatting.

Does it work on an iPad?

The export works on a computer or an iPad. Installing the bookmark is a little more fiddly on an iPad — you bookmark any page, then edit the bookmark and paste in the address. A desktop browser is the smooth path.

What if there are thousands of highlights?

That's the normal case, and it's expected. The book is split into parts — Old Testament, New Testament, and so on — so even a very large collection stays organized and printable.

Is this an official Church product?

No. Their Testament is an independent project with no connection to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints or its Gospel Library app. It simply reads the annotations you've already made and typesets them.
