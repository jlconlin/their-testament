Their Testament

A typeset keepsake of the scriptures someone marked.

Their Testament turns a lifetime of Gospel Library highlights, margin notes, and tags into a printed-quality PDF — every marked verse shown in context, in its original highlight color, with the notes set in the margin beside it.

It runs entirely in your browser. Your annotations are never uploaded to a server — this site doesn't have one.

Step one — get your annotations

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:
    📖 Export my annotations (drag it — don't click it here)
  2. Go to churchofjesuschrist.org and sign in. Open anything under Study — a chapter, a talk, your notebook.
  3. Click the Export my annotations bookmark. A small panel appears and pages through your notes.
  4. When it finishes it saves a file called their-testament-annotations-date.json to your downloads. That file is your data — keep it safe.

Step two — make the book

The in-browser book generator is still being built. For now, send your .json file to whoever is putting the keepsake together and they'll typeset it for you.

Is this private?

Yes. The bookmarklet reads only your own annotations, using the session you're already signed in to, and hands them straight to your browser's download. Nothing is transmitted to this site or anywhere else. The saved .json file stays on your computer until you choose to share it.
